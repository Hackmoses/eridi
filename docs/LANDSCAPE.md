# Landscape

Products near Eridi, checked November 2026. Keep this current; agents should read it before
proposing features, so Eridi stays different rather than becoming a copy.

| Product | What it does | How Eridi differs |
|---|---|---|
| **BenchMind** (benchmind.app) | Lab knowledge hub: "store protocols, files, notes, and project decisions in one place." Lab plan around $80/month for 10 seats. | Eridi doesn't ask labs to move files. It indexes them where they already live, detects changes automatically, keeps the record in an open format in the lab's own storage, and exposes it to any AI assistant. |
| **LabTrail** (labtrail.ai) | "Agentic lab intelligence" that connects a testing lab's systems and answers questions about its data. | Aimed at testing and diagnostic labs' operational data. Eridi focuses on research decisions: why protocols and analyses changed, and whether it worked. |
| **Iranti** | Persistent memory layer for AI coding agents. | Software developers, not research labs. Shows that "memory for AI" is a growing category. |
| **Benchling, Labguru, LabArchives** (ELNs) | Electronic lab notebooks and lab data platforms. | They hold what's entered into them. Eridi works across all tools, including ELN exports, and captures the "why" between versions. Integrate with them; don't compete on notebook features. |
| **Microsoft Copilot, Google Drive search, Spotlight** | Find files and summarize them. | They find files. They don't hold the reasons behind changes or their outcomes, and they don't cross storage providers. |

## Positioning in one line

The lab's record of what changed, why, and whether it worked, built from the files it already
has, owned by the lab, and usable by any AI.

## Rules for building

- Don't build features whose main value is "store everything in Eridi." That's BenchMind's path.
- Do build anything that makes capture effortless where people already work, or makes the
  record more useful to people and AI assistants.
